Saju Today: Daily Fortune
Your daily fortune and Saju reading, written by AI for reflection and fun.

Saju Today turns your birth date and time into a short daily fortune reading — a personal Saju reading, written fresh each morning, in plain language you can actually sit with.

Saju (사주), also known as the Four Pillars or BaZi, is a traditional East Asian reading system built from the year, month, day, and hour of your birth. Where a horoscope gives one reading to everyone born in the same month, Saju starts from your own four pillars. Saju Today reads those pillars for you and writes up what they suggest about the shape of your day.

WHAT'S INSIDE

• Today — a short daily fortune on the shape of your day, plus one takeaway you can actually use.
• Saju x Stars — Eastern Saju and Western astrology read side by side. Where the two skies agree, and where they split. This is the part you won't find in a normal horoscope app.
• This Week — the seven-day flow, its main theme, your good days and your careful days.
• Compatibility — the energy between two people, read from both birth charts.
• My Chart — your four pillars, your core element, and how the five elements sit in your chart.
• Your reading history stays in the app, so you can look back at how the themes shifted.
• A quiet, dark, uncluttered design made for reading. No feed to scroll, and no ads anywhere in the app.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Enter your birth date and birth time (solar calendar).
2. Saju Today builds your four pillars chart and works out the elemental balance.
3. Each day, a fresh reading is written from that chart.
4. Read it, keep what is useful, and leave the rest.

Don't know your exact birth hour? Use 12:00 — you only lose a little resolution on one of the four pillars.

WRITTEN BY AI — AND WE SAY SO

Every reading in Saju Today is generated by an AI language model (Google Gemini) applying traditional Saju concepts to your chart. Nothing is written by a human fortune teller, and we don't pretend otherwise.

That means the readings are for reflection and entertainment. They are not a prediction of the future, and they are not medical, psychological, legal, or financial advice. If a line lands, take it as a prompt to think about — nothing more. Please make real decisions with real information, and speak to a qualified professional when a decision matters.

A NOTE ON WHAT THIS IS

Saju is a centuries-old tradition, and it is a way of framing a day rather than a set of instructions for living one. Saju Today is built in that spirit: a small, calm reading to start the morning with, and a reminder that the interesting part is what you make of it.
